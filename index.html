About Us

Ella Kate Events began with a simple dream: to turn special moments into lifelong memories. We believe every event—no matter how big or small—deserves to feel magical, meaningful, and completely *you*. Our passion lies in the little details, the thoughtful touches, and the creative spark that brings your vision to life.

What started as helping friends plan birthdays and bridal showers quickly grew into something bigger. Today, we’re proud to offer personalized event planning for baby showers, bachelorette parties, birthdays, and more. We listen closely, plan intentionally, and work tirelessly to make sure your event feels effortless, joyful, and unforgettable.

At Ella Kate Events, you're not just a client—you’re a collaborator, a friend, and the inspiration behind every choice we make. Let’s create something beautiful together.
